# Product

## Vision
Every Ecuadorian worker can understand the IESS retirement rules and know, in a few minutes, what pension they would receive today and what it takes to reach the pension they want.

## Purpose
A public landing page for Ecuadorian workers that explains IESS retirement rules and lets them estimate their pension and how much more they need to contribute to reach a target pension. The project also serves as a practice repository where non-technical team members (project managers, business analysts) learn to contribute through pull requests.
